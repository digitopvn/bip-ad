BuildInPublic

Why 3:12

Read the clock like a display. It says BIP.

BIP is Build In Public, the habit this board is made for. 3:12 is also the moment that counts: every board locks into a permanent record, and the products holding the Top 10 go into that night's video.

  • One lock for all three boards. Daily, Weekly and Monthly snapshot together at 3:12 ICT.
  • Snapshots never change. Each night is kept as a permanent record you can link to.
  • The clock is strict. A payment confirmed at 3:13 moves you on the live board now and counts for the next night's video.

How it works

Your agent does the listing. You approve the money.

Products are created only through MCP. The website is where people discover them, and where you approve checkout.

  1. Connect

    Add bip.ad to Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client and sign in once.

    https://bip.ad/mcp

  2. List

    Your agent drafts the listing and builds the page from safe widgets. Moderation reviews it.

    products.create
    pages.set_blocks

  3. Bid

    It quotes a Lucky Boost. You approve the payment in PayPal or SePay.

    boosts.quote
    boosts.checkout

  4. Go live at 3:12

    Hold a Top 10 seat at the lock and your product is in that night's video.

    snapshot · 3:12 ICT

Rules

Pay-to-win, and we say so up front.

The highest active bid always ranks first. Everything else only breaks ties. Every confirmed bid is kept, win or lose.

  1. Highest active bidAlways decides
  2. Owner's successful referralsTies only
  3. Valid unique clicks, last 30 daysTies only
  4. Valid unique views, last 30 daysTies only
  5. First to reach the amountTies only

A $10 bid with no referrals still ranks above a $9 bid with 100 referrals. Free listings always sit below paid ones.

What a bid buys

  • A higher seat while your bid is active
  • A place in the video if you hold the Top 10 at 3:12
  • Reach from your board's shared ads fund

What it never buys

  • Guaranteed impressions, clicks or sales
  • Keeping your seat until the lock
  • A refund for being outbid

Transparency

80% of every bid buys ads for its own board.

Daily money never pays for Weekly ads. Spend caps are enforced in code, and every spend is published with its source and evidence.

Before you bid.

Do I get money back if I am outbid?

No. bip.ad is all-pay: every confirmed bid is kept, even if it finishes outside the Top 10. Refunds cover only failed or duplicate payments, fraud, legal requirements or our own error.

Does a bid guarantee traffic or sales?

No. A bid buys position while it is active. Impressions, clicks and sales are never guaranteed, and the ads fund is shared by the whole board.

How long does a bid stay active?

From the moment the payment is confirmed: 24 hours on Daily, 7 days on Weekly and 30 days on Monthly. Each extra bid is its own contribution and expires on its own clock.

Which payments do you accept?

PayPal in USD and SePay in VND with VietQR. Your rank moves only after the provider confirms the payment through a verified webhook, never from a redirect or a screenshot.

Can free products appear on the board?

Yes, always below paid ones, ordered by the owner's successful referrals and then valid clicks. If fewer than ten products have bids, free products fill the Top 10.
