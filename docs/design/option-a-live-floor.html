Straight answers.

Do I get money back if I am outbid?

No. bid.ad is all-pay: every confirmed bid is kept, even if it finishes outside the Top 10. Refunds cover only failed or duplicate payments, fraud, legal requirements or our own error.

Does a bid guarantee traffic or sales?

No. A bid buys position while it is active. Impressions, clicks and sales are never guaranteed, and the ads fund is shared by the whole board.

When exactly is the snapshot taken?

Every day at 03:12 in Vietnam (UTC+7). Payments confirmed before that moment count. A confirmation that arrives after 03:12 counts for the next night, even if you paid a minute earlier.

Can free products appear on the board?

Yes. Free products always rank below paid ones and are ordered by the owner's successful referrals, then valid clicks and views. If fewer than ten products have bids, free products fill the Top 10.

Why can I only list products through MCP?

Every page is built from validated widgets with no custom scripts, which keeps listings safe to browse. The website is for discovery, checkout and your account.

What counts as a successful referral?

A real, separate account you referred that completes a qualifying paid bid and passes fraud checks. Each person counts once, no matter how many times they bid. The hold period and payout minimum are listed in the referral terms.
